Program name: Iron House Gym Pass Follow-Up

What it is

When you request a free 3-day pass on ironhousejax.com and enter your phone number, we send you the pass by text and follow up about using it. After your first visit we may text you about membership options.

How you sign up

On the free 3-day pass form at ironhousejax.com you enter your name, phone number and email. Below the phone field there is a checkbox that is not ticked by default. It reads:

"Text me my pass. I agree to receive texts from Iron House Gym about my pass and membership. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. See SMS Terms."

You are only signed up for text messages if you tick that box yourself. Ticking it is optional and is not required to get your pass; if you leave it unticked you still receive your pass and we never text you. This is the only way to sign up. We do not buy, rent or import phone numbers.

Message frequency

Varies. No more than five messages for a single pass request. Messages stop once you visit or join.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for the messages; your carrier may.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message and you will be unsubscribed immediately. You will get one confirmation message and nothing after that.

Getting help

Reply HELP, call (904) 329-1040, or email ironhousejacksonville@gmail.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed.

Supported carriers

Most major US carriers.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your phone number or SMS consent with third parties for their marketing.
